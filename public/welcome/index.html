How the PrayerEngine works

Five steps, all of them on your device, none of them inventing doctrine on your behalf.

  1. 01

    It reads for safety first

    Before anything is composed, a conservative classifier looks for crisis, acute distress and metaphysical-escalation signals. Anything it flags stops the engine and returns fixed wording written by people.

  2. 02

    It finds the intention

    Your words are scored against a researched taxonomy of needs. Choose an intention yourself and that choice always wins.

  3. 03

    It filters the library

    Candidates are narrowed by form and length, ranked for the tone and language you prefer, and anything matching a word or image you asked to avoid is removed outright.

  4. 04

    It draws without repeating

    Every compatible item is offered once before any of them come round again — and a new cycle never opens with the one you just received.

  5. 05

    It forgets almost everything

    What you typed is never stored. Only content IDs and cycle counters stay behind, in your browser, and you can clear them whenever you like.
