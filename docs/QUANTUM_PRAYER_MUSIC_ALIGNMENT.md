# Quantum Prayer and reviewed-listening alignment

Review date: 23 July 2026

Method version: `quantum-prayer-v1`

Catalogue snapshot: 153 records, eight public selections

## Executive decision

LumenNous now implements the source Quantum Prayer method as a versioned,
three-minute **Embodied Coherence Prayer**. The order, core timing, breathing
count, threefold intention and five daily applications are retained. Claims
that consciousness collapses external reality, transmits through quantum
entanglement or changes probability fields are not retained as science.

The app uses “quantum” as contemplative metaphor. The operational method is
breath regulation, body awareness, gentle emotional congruence, clear prayer,
gratitude, release and a grounded next action.

Music is integrated by purpose, acoustic character, cultural meaning and
listener choice—not by advertised healing frequencies. The complete
153-record YouTube catalogue remains available to the editorial audit
pipeline. Only eight explicitly allowlisted, low-claim-risk records reach the
public app, and each prayer session remains complete in silence.

## Source methodology

### Core principles

The PDF describes prayer as “alignment” or “tuning” across breath, body,
emotion, attention and intention. Its practical principles are:

- regulate before speaking;
- begin in present bodily experience;
- bring the desired inner quality into felt awareness;
- state one clear intention;
- repeat it slowly enough to inhabit the words;
- release rather than grip the outcome;
- let gratitude close the practice.

The intended outcomes are a calmer starting state, embodied presence, clearer
attention, emotionally congruent prayer, receptivity, less urgency and more
coherent action. These are suitable app outcomes because they concern the
practitioner’s relationship to the moment rather than a promised external
event.

### Exact source sequence

| Stage | Source instruction | Core time |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Breath regulation | Inhale four counts, hold two, exhale six | 60 seconds |
| Body awareness | Notice feet, posture, shoulders and jaw | 30 seconds |
| Emotional activation | Feel the intended quality as already present | 30 seconds |
| Intention statement | Speak aloud or silently, three times | 30 seconds |
| Release and receive | Gratitude and non-gripping | 30 seconds |

The PDF applies this core to morning setting, midday recentering, evening
integration, a challenge reset and before sleep. All five are implemented as
static, reviewable session templates.

### Deliberate safety adaptations

The following changes are intentional rather than accidental drift:

- The 4–2–6 pattern is an optional timed adaptation of the source’s *counts*.
  Natural breathing and a no-hold option are always valid.
- “Emotional activation” is renamed **Evoke** and is skippable. A faint,
  believable quality or a neutral sensation is enough; positivity is never
  forced.
- “Already answered,” “do not doubt” and “it is done” become honest intention
  plus non-gripping. This avoids turning uncertainty, grief or illness into a
  failure of belief.
- Release includes one grounded action where useful. Prayer accompanies
  practical care; it does not replace it.
- Intercession respects the other person’s autonomy and cannot claim to alter
  their field, decisions or outcome.
- The PDF’s appearance, metabolism, weight-loss, “perfect health,” cellular
  memory and guaranteed healing language is excluded.

## Scientific and editorial boundary

The PDF moves from real quantum concepts to claims that personal consciousness
collapses reality, prayer travels non-locally through entanglement, and
coherent intention reorganises probability. Those inferences are not
established physical mechanisms. A large confirmatory quantum random-number
study with 12,571 participants supported its null model rather than reliable
intentional influence ([Frontiers in Psychology / PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5872141/)).

The app therefore separates:

- **spiritual source and metaphor**: possibility, participation, surrender and
  mystery;
- **research-aligned practice**: slow breathing, attention, body awareness,
  meaning, music preference and relaxation;
- **unsupported mechanism**: thought controlling quantum events, healing
  frequencies, DNA repair, manifestation guarantees or distant control.

Slow-breathing evidence is relevant but not universal or diagnostic. A
systematic review of 15 studies in healthy participants found
psychophysiological associations and substantial variation in protocols; it
does not validate every breathing ratio or guarantee calm
([Zaccaro et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6137615/)).

## Music research conclusions

### What can reasonably be said

- Music interventions can reduce anxiety during studied treatment periods,
  but effects vary and follow-up evidence is less certain. A 2021
  meta-analysis included 32 randomised studies and 1,924 participants; that
  evidence concerns interventions, not every public video
  ([Lu et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34365216/)).
- Natural sounds can support positive affect or reduce stress and annoyance in
  some contexts, with substantial heterogeneity
  ([Buxton et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8040792/)).
- Active chanting or recitation can pace breath and give ritual and social
  meaning. Its value cannot be reduced to a carrier frequency.
- Silence is not a failed condition. It may be preferable for speech,
  discernment, sensory sensitivity, acute stress or language-heavy work.

### What remains limited or population-specific

- Binaural-beat protocols and findings are heterogeneous; reliable brainwave
  entrainment is not established
  ([systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10198548/)).
- A 2024 meta-analysis found a small laboratory-task benefit from white/pink
  noise in ADHD or elevated-symptom groups, a negative effect in non-ADHD
  comparison groups, and no brown-noise studies. This does not justify a
  universal focus track
  ([Nigg et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11283987/)).
- Singing-bowl studies are promising to some reviewers but heterogeneous;
  assessed randomised trials in one 2025 review were at high risk of bias.
  Bowls are labelled experiential/preliminary and have a sensory opt-out
  ([systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12063014/)).
- Continuous ocean, rain or noise recordings may mask interruptions, but they
  are not presented as insomnia treatment.

### Claims excluded from recommendations

- 432 Hz as a natural or cosmic tuning with special healing power;
- 528 Hz for DNA repair, cellular regeneration or transformation;
- Solfeggio tones as an ancient clinically effective frequency system;
- chakra frequencies that unblock or cleanse;
- brown noise as an established ADHD treatment;
- 40 Hz YouTube audio as an Alzheimer’s intervention;
- any track that diagnoses, detoxifies, manifests or cures.

## Public selection policy

A record can appear publicly only when it is both:

1. eligible under conservative checks—approved family, embedded playback,
   `ready_for_editorial_review`, low claim risk, acceptable evidence label and
   acceptable rights state; and
2. explicitly named in the human-reviewed publication allowlist.

This fails closed. Pending, blocked, claim-heavy, frequency-led, DNA, chakra,
Solfeggio and binaural records never reach `/listen` or a session.

| Before Sleep | `NATURE-011` ocean | `NATURE-014` rainforest | Speakers and comfortable level; external listening needs YouTube’s or the device’s timer; no overnight earbuds |

Audio is selected before the timer and can continue under the session. It is
not phase-synchronised: third-party embeds cannot provide reliable,
advertisement-free, seamless timing. Only one in-page player remains loaded,
the session includes a stop-music control, and the player is unloaded when the
three-minute session ends. Spoken intention remains primary.

## Safety, access and privacy

- Every session works without sound and without closed eyes.
- Breath holding is never required. The player says to stop counting if dizzy,
  air-hungry or panicky.
- Breath focus can be replaced entirely by visual and contact-point grounding.
- The emotional stage may be skipped without shame.
- No prayer guarantees healing or another person’s response.
- Intercession cannot override autonomy; practical help remains available.
- No third-party player request occurs before explicit consent.
- Use the lowest comfortable level, take breaks, stop for discomfort or
  tinnitus, and never run a guided session while driving. WHO guidance
  emphasises that level, duration and repeated exposure jointly determine
  hearing risk
  ([WHO safe listening](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/deafness-and-hearing-loss-safe-listening)).

## Remaining release gates

The implementation is technically validated, but seed spiritual content
remains marked `draft` pending human editorial approval. Before a public
release, an editor should:

- confirm each selected video is still available and embeddable;
- record a stronger rights basis than “likely official” where possible;
- review chant language and cultural/theological context;
- listen through every selected recording for sudden dynamics, speech,
  advertising and unsuitable lyrical content;
- recheck health and support links on a dated schedule;
- commission or license first-party audio if seamless, dependable session
  sound is required.

The app should continue to call this **reviewed listening** or
**music-supported prayer**, not music therapy.
